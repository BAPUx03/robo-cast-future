# About Page Redesign

## Goal
Make the About page feel distinct from the rest of the website while preserving Modtech’s existing brand, company facts, images, navigation, and calls to action.

## What will change
- Replace the current generic page banner with an image-led editorial introduction built specifically for Modtech’s story.
- Turn the four company statistics into a horizontal proof strip integrated with the opening section.
- Present Establishment, Projects, and The Concept as a numbered company journey instead of repeated cards.
- Give Investment Casting and Robotics & Automation separate full-width visual chapters with alternating image and text layouts.
- Rework “Why Modtech” into a prominent service promise section with clearer scanning and hierarchy.
- Replace the capability card grid with a compact engineering capability index.
- Simplify the customer section into a cleaner trust wall and finish with stronger division/contact actions.
- Ensure the redesigned page remains polished on mobile and desktop, with restrained scroll reveals and accessible contrast.

## Technical details
- Keep the existing global header, footer, theme system, company data, and route.
- Rebuild only the About page presentation using current semantic colour tokens and uploaded machinery imagery.
- Use existing shared controls for actions and avoid adding new backend behavior.
- Verify page rendering, mobile layout, links, images, and the final build.
